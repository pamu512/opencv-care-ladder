# Care Ladder Vision, VO v5 (plain spoken English)

Target: ~2:20–2:40. Same scrubbed visuals. No code identifiers spoken.
Opener per Anoop 2026-10-06: no "My mother lives alone in India"; short first-person context only.

## Beat 1, Why / open
After several falls earlier this year, we needed a way to keep an eye on my mother without invading her privacy.
I built Care Ladder.
It turns a home camera into something that checks in before it escalates,
with blurred clips so she never feels watched.
Here's the live screen.
The buttons are ready to try, including a fall.

## Beat 2, She's fine
One simple care plan for the home: a quiet zone, how long stillness can last, and what to try next.
It looks again to be sure, then a speaker in the room asks if she's okay.
If she says she's fine, nobody gets bothered.
No call goes out.
The timeline shows why. What the camera saw changed what happened next.

## Beat 3, Silence / family
Same quiet, but this time she doesn't answer.
After the spoken check-in, the family chat gets a message.
Anyone can tap to say they've got it.
The ladder stands down.
Same plan. Different next step.

## Beat 4, Privacy + honesty
A real photo is checked for a person.
Then privacy: only a soft outline of her reaches the caregiver.
Blur happens before anything is stored.
We tested this on eleven cases, small, not clinical.
It confirms in about eighteen seconds, and it didn't raise a false alarm in those eleven.
A fall detector is in the project for research,
but it isn't what decides here. The camera checks are.
It never calls emergency services.

## Beat 5, Fall path
On the demo, you can try a fall.
On the research video, she is upright, then she is on the floor.
The card says it thinks she may have fallen.
The picture stays blurred.
Before it escalates, it can look again at what it just saw, a second opinion.
Cloud analysis can take minutes, so for judges we show a fall that already finished.
This is a research re-enactment, not a clinical study.

## Beat 6, Close
It runs in the cloud for this live demo.
Care Ladder: the camera notices, the ladder decides, every step is written down.
Confirm before you escalate.
The project and the live demo are on screen.
