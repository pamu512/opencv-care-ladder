Ask first. Escalate last.

Most monitoring treats stillness as an emergency. Care Ladder treats it as a question: asked out loud, answered by Pat, and only escalated when nobody answers.

Built on OpenCV cues and a configurable ladder of rungs, every incident leaves a complete, ordered audit trail: what was seen, what was asked, who was told, and where it stopped.

The vocabulary

Honest cues. Zero guesses.

The vision layer says only what it can defend. Each cue is a structured event with a detector source on the timeline; a covered lens stays camera health, never distress.

01 no_movement Stillness. A person-like blob in the plan zone, still past the configured timeout. The timeout itself adapts to the household’s learned routine.
02 no_visibility Absence. The monitored person leaves, or cannot be seen in the zone.
03 camera_occlusion Covered lens. Lens covered or unreadable: the ladder asks to clear it and informs family on a camera-health basis. No distress claim, no dial.
04 distress_heuristic Motion heuristic. A simple motion/pose heuristic, explicitly not a medical assessment.
05 opencv_dnn_person Person detection. An optional fifth source: a real photo through the MediaPipe person-detection ONNX via OpenCV 5 DNN. Frames attach as silhouettes, the audit cue tagged with the detector name.
The walk-down

One rung at a time, in order.

An incident walks the configured rungs of demo_home.yaml. Replies branch the path; acknowledges stand it down; every move appends an audit event.

Rung 02–03 · Confirm & ask

Re-perceive, then check in.

Re-check before acting. Then a home-speaker check-in: “Are you okay?” or “could you clear the lens?” when the cue is occlusion.

Rung 04 · Listen

A bounded wait.

A settle window, interruptible at any moment by an acknowledge: from the console, the family page, or a Telegram reply.

Rung 05 · Inform

Family gets a card, not a panic.

A BotThread inform card (1|2|3) via Telegram or the console fallback. Status language stays calm: Care plan active · Checking on Pat · Camera blocked · Resolved.

Rung 06 · Call

Stub dialer, fiction numbers.

Caregiver, then secondary: scripted outcomes only. The emergency rung above it stays fail-closed and disabled by default.

Branching · what the answers decide

“I’m fine” resolves. Silence walks. Help jumps.

  • clear_ok: “I’m fine,” “don’t worry” → resolved at check-in; no dial, no emergency.
  • needs_human: help or mixed hurt → jumps to notify and dial; the ladder never invents an OK.
  • unclear: silence, a groan → continues down the ladder, jumps logged.
  • family ack: button, webhook, or 1|2|3 → resolved as caregiver_ack; remaining rungs skipped.
  • dial answered → resolve · no-answer escalates to the next dial with logged jumps.
Privacy & safety

What the family sees is a silhouette.

Frames cannot attach to an incident as raw video: the attach path itself maps them through a Gaussian blur or a filled silhouette first, and stamps the audit cue with the proof.

  • EnforcedAt attach, in code. A non-zero pre-event frame count without the privacy flag is refused outright.
  • StoredPre-event clips land in S3 as silhouettes. The live stack keeps no raw identifiable video.
  • DialedDemo contacts are reserved fiction (NANP 555-01XX); emergency-like numbers are rejected at plan load.
  • Fail-closedThe emergency rung is disabled by default and audits only: no code path places a real 911 call.

Honest limits

The short answers, kept short.

Is this a medical device?

No. It claims no clinical diagnosis. The distress cue is a simple motion/pose heuristic, and the spoken-reply classifier is deterministic; an optional LLM path stays off unless explicitly enabled.

Does it ever call 911?

Never. The emergency rung is fail-closed: off by default, audits-only even if enabled. The dialer is a stub; demo phones are reserved fiction (555-01XX); emergency-like numbers are rejected at plan load.

Is anything actually live?

The deployment is: CloudFront → ALB → ECS Fargate, DynamoDB incident store, S3 silhouette clips, EventBridge cue bus. The inputs are not: speaker and dial are simulators, cues arrive from fixtures or controlled frames. CI tests and rolls every push to main; infra notes have the details.

What happens overnight?

Quiet hours soft-suppress non-distress cues: stillness and absence get a suppress event instead of waking the house. Distress cues still run the ladder.
